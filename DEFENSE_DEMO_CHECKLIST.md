# Current defense rehearsal checklist

Use the current APK and https://harmonia-47c73.web.app. The detailed presenter guide is in `output/pdf/harmonia-defense-cheatsheet.pdf`.

1. Assign the intended active student to the reviewing coach. Rehearse on a physical phone in the intended room.
2. Create a comfortable Beginner single-note exercise, approximately 15 seconds with no prerequisite. For scales/songs, inspect and approve timed target notes first.
3. Sign in on Android with internet and microphone permission. Play the reference, stop playback, then record solo singing.
4. Show original pitch and experimental clarity/steadiness indicators. Explain unavailable measurements rather than treating them as zero.
5. After actual upload, use the website to play the student recording and save feedback/corrected scores.
6. Select **Approve advancement** or **Request repeat**. Confirming a score or finalizing a general assessment does not independently advance the student.
7. Let Android receive the decision; verify a dependent exercise unlocks only with explicit approval. Demonstrate already saved recordings/received feedback offline.

Current policies: Beginner +/-50 cents/pass 60%; Intermediate +/-35/pass 70%; Advanced +/-25/pass 80%. Recording supports 3-600 seconds, 65-1050 Hz, compressed M4A uploads capped at 8 MiB. Tone is an experimental programmed indicator, not a second trained model. No measured real-singer 80-90% accuracy is established.

If interrupted, reopen the same exercise and recover an eligible saved recording. Preserve local files if upload fails. A genuine previous attempt can be used as a clearly labeled backup demonstration.

Recorded automated checks used generated audio for repeatable pitch expectations. See `AI_IMPLEMENTATION_STATUS.md` and `HarmoniaAppMobile/docs/ai-validation.md`; these do not replace physical-phone singing or independent accuracy evaluation.
